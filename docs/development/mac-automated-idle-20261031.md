2026-10-31; SRC-0578 / TEST-0126. The linker map places the own MWP1 v3 trace at `00000BAC`; selected read-only memory-view rows are preserved as [originally observed bytes](../evidence/traces/2026-10-31/mac-idle-and-automated.json). They are not a complete 128-byte dump. No Apple ROM, disk, shared folder or physical hardware was used.

After an initial interval without deliberate mouse actions inside the guest scene, the paused trace showed 13,887 services, one baseline, **zero losses**, zero deliveries, and pointer (256,171). After the memory panel was closed, the guest resumed, one SKY window-relative drag from (500,450) to (501,450) was issued, and the toolbar was used to pause it. The second snapshot showed 29,098 services, 126 baselines, **140 total losses**, one queued sample, zero deliveries, and pointer still (256,171). These are two cumulative snapshots in one run: +15,211 services and +140 losses. Host-window setup, resumption, pointer repositioning and pausing can also contribute events; no exact gesture or real-device motion is inferred.

The retained first post-idle loss has reason 6 (Gray phase jump), snapshots `00EF4C4C` and `00AF4C4C`, and packed predecessor `A9000000`; the first-loss field does not classify the remaining losses. The two snapshots differ in PB bit 6, outside the stable-input mask, so this particular record is not the earlier unstable-pair reason 2. The zero-loss idle observation and later losses motivate investigating signal timing and sample coherence but do not identify the failing layer or license invented movement. They are not a successful GUI drag, physical Macintosh or release pass. The owned Snow process closed after 148.33 host seconds, within its 240-second watchdog; the ROM hash was unchanged.
